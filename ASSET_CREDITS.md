The Galactic Center background is a NASA/JPL-Caltech Spitzer image, from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Galactic_Center_Of_Milky_Way..jpg).
Its source page identifies the image as public domain in the United States.
The image was resized for use in the offline game bundle.

The Tex and enemy dinosaur sprites were created for this game.
